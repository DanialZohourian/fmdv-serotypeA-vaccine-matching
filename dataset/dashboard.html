Foot-and-mouth disease virus, serotype A

Each row is one lab test: does this vaccine serum stop this field virus?

The file pairs.csv holds 916 of those tests. A number called r1 says how close the field virus is to the vaccine. At 0.30 or above, labs call it a match. Below that, the vaccine is a poor match.

One virus, three answers

This is a real row group from Singanallur and colleagues, 2022. The same Iranian field virus, A/IRN/10/2018, was put against three vaccine serums. That is why one virus can appear many times.

Field virus Vaccine serum r1 Call
A/IRN/10/2018 A/Iran-05 0.48 Match
same virus A22/Iraq 0.45 Match
same virus A/ASIA/G-VII below 0.07 Poor match

The G-VII vaccine, built for a different lineage, did not neutralise this Iran-05 virus. That contrast is why those 15 pairs are tagged holdout_cross_lineage and kept out of ordinary training.

The years are not even

Almost all exact r1 values are from 2001–2009. Then the numeric record goes quiet.

2000–2004291 tests · 18% match
2005–2009243 tests · 28% match
2010–20140 tests
2015–201917 tests · 47% match
2020–202467 tests · 30% match
202516 tests · 38% match

The empty years are not a biological fact. World Reference Laboratory reports from about 2009 to 2021, including most later Iranian samples, are scanned pages. Their columns could not be read safely, so they were left out. SIS-10, SIS-12 and FAR-11 are thin in this table for that reason.

Looking at the protein does not separate the two groups cleanly

For 288 exact neutralisation pairs, both the field virus and the vaccine have a VP1 protein sequence. VP1 is the main outer protein. The distance below is the share of amino acids that differ.

Matched pairs

10.5%

average amino-acid difference · 56 pairs

Poor matches

12.2%

average amino-acid difference · 232 pairs

The poor matches are only a little more different. A model that only counts “how many letters changed” will mix the two groups. That is the reason the research plan looks at particular positions, including the GH loop, instead of one distance number.

pairs.csv

The tests. Use rows marked main_vnt to train. Keep lpbe_covariate apart unless the assay type is a feature. Leave holdout_cross_lineage for a later test.

viruses.csv

210 viruses, with country, year, lineage and sub-lineage when a paper stated them. Many viruses are listed here even when their r1 lived only in a bar chart.

features.csv

425 pairs with a VP1 distance, a count of changes in residues 140–160, and a count of N-glycosylation motifs. 69 GenBank records sit in sequences.csv.

What this page is not claiming

  • Bar charts in Upadhyaya 2014 and Bari 2014 were not turned into numbers. Inventing r1 from a picture would poison the table.
  • 158 scanned laboratory PDFs are listed in scanned_reports_not_digitized.txt and are not in the 916.
  • Azimi 2020 printed seven Iranian isolates and then six r1 values. Those six were not assigned. Only Qom B3 = 0.19 was explicit enough to keep.
  • Some vaccine sequences are the closest public record, not the exact serum batch used in the test. The note is in sequences.csv.
